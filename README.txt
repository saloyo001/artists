KENYAN ARTISANS - ARTIST UPLOAD DEMO

Files: index.html, style.css, script.js

HOW TO USE
1. Open this folder in VS Code.
2. Open index.html in your browser, or use the Live Server extension.
3. Click Sell your art / I'm an artisan, then choose Go to Artist Studio. You can also click Artist Studio in the navigation.
4. Fill out the artwork title, price, category, artist name, location, photo and description. Click Publish artwork.
5. The new item appears in Explore Art and in Your listings on this browser.
6. Use Remove listing to delete it.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
This is a front-end demo. It starts with no product listings. Products and photos are saved in localStorage in the current browser only. They do not appear on another device or for other website visitors. For a real marketplace where artists can log in and share listings with all customers, the site needs a backend server/database, authentication, image storage and secure payment/order handling.

Use an image smaller than 1.5 MB for the demo.
